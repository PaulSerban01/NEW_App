Component

Chip & badge

Inline status pill for short labels. Five variants cover the common semantic intents (neutral, accent, success, warning, danger) plus an optional leading dot. Pattern based on Tailwind Plus badges, themed with design-system tokens.

Variants

Neutral Accent Success Warning Danger

The neutral variant matches the Tailwind Plus “gray” badge and is the default choice when the badge content isn’t inherently positive or negative (e.g. a generic status like “În cultură” or “Active”).

With leading dot

For unread/online indicators or visual emphasis on the badge color.

Idle Online Pending Offline

In context — list row

Neutral badge used on the parcels list to display agricultural status.
